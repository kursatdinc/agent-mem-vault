# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is incorporated here by reference. Everyone taking part in the project's issues, discussions,
pull requests and wiki is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept feedback graciously, and keep the
discussion about the work. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a violation, open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/kursatdinc/agent-mem-vault/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches only the maintainer) or contact the maintainer, [@kursatdinc](https://github.com/kursatdinc),
through GitHub. For something that does not need to stay private, open an
[issue](https://github.com/kursatdinc/agent-mem-vault/issues).

The maintainer reviews every report, respects the privacy of the reporter, and applies the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant: a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a
permanent ban, in proportion to the behavior.
